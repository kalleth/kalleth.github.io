I'm an engineering leader based in the south-east of the UK who delivers high-quality software projects, predominantly using Ruby on Rails, Golang and JavaScript.

About me

I've worked in technology since I was 16, spending the last 10 years in engineering leadership.

Since 2023, I've worked for Relay Commerce, Inc as their CTO. Before that, I spent a few years as co-founder of TEAMS.gg, a gaming startup helping players find the right teammates for online games.

In my spare time, I enjoy scuba diving and volunteering with young people as a Scout Leader with the UK Scout Association.
